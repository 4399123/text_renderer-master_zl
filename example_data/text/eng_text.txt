Seconds before Earth is demolished to make way for a galactic freeway, Arthur Dent is plucked off the planet by his friend Ford Prefect, a researcher for the revised edition of The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy who, for the last fifteen years, has been posing as an out-of-work actor.
Together, this dynamic pair began a journey through space aided by a galaxyful of fellow travelers: Zaphod Beeblebrox, the two-headed, three-armed, ex-hippie and totally out-to-lunch president of the galaxy; Trillian (formerly Tricia McMillan), Zaphod's girlfriend, whom Arthur tried to pick up at a cocktail party once upon a time zone; Marvin, a paranoid, brilliant, and chronically depressed robot; and Veet Voojagig, a former graduate student obsessed with the disappearance of all the ballpoint pens he's bought over the years.
Where are these pens? Why are we born? Why do we die? For all the answers, stick your thumb to the stars!
Rooted in Shanghai (Shencheng) and expanding nationwide, Alibaba Group stands as a benchmark enterprise of China’s digital economy. Founded in Hangzhou in 1999 by eighteen co-founders with the original mission of “making it easy to do business anywhere”, the conglomerate has evolved over more than two decades into an integrated business ecosystem spanning e-commerce, cloud computing, local life services, cross-border trade and smart logistics. Shanghai, also known as Shencheng, serves as Alibaba’s pivotal strategic hub for its Yangtze River Delta layout and nationwide digital expansion.
Shanghai has become Alibaba’s key base outside its Hangzhou headquarters for high-end industrial deployment and cutting-edge R&D. Having encountered setbacks during its early days in Shanghai, the company has since set up business clusters across Xuhui Riverside, Hongqiao Central Business District, Pudong Zhangjiang and Putuo Taopu districts. Its Shanghai headquarters sits along Xuhui West Bund, housing core operation teams for Taobao and Tmall. The Hongqiao Alibaba Center accommodates over 5,000 employees and builds a Yangtze River Delta e-commerce cluster leveraging Hongqiao’s superior transport links. The DAMO Academy and T-Head Semiconductor Research Institute settle on Pudong’s AI Island to advance forward-looking research on large language models, cloud computing and artificial intelligence, with much of the R&D for Qwen multimodal large models carried out locally.
Furthermore, Alibaba forged a new retail partnership with Bailian Group, a time-honored local retail giant in Shanghai. Ele.me is deeply entrenched in the city’s food service and instant retail market, while a digital agricultural supply chain base in Putuo connects agricultural producers with fresh produce retailers. Alipay has been widely embedded in Shanghai’s public transit and municipal government service scenarios, facilitating the city’s comprehensive digital upgrade.
